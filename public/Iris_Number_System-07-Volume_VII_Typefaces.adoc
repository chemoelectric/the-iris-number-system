A Rigorous Application of the Counting-Iris Number System and the Master Field Equation to Discrete Coordinate Curves, Multi-Aperture Font Outlines, and Digital Typeface Design

== Prelude: Foundations of Digital Typeface Design in the Iris Framework

Digital typeface design and font outline geometry operate inherently within discrete coordinate grids and multiscale rasterization apertures. This volume establishes the rigorous mathematical and algorithmic principles of digital letterform design, curve formulation, and discrete metric rasterization within the Counting-Iris framework.
