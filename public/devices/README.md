# Experimental Devices & Hardware Blueprints

This directory contains first-principles engineering blueprints, physical derivations, mechanical drawings, and solid-state circuit schematics for original, non-conventional physical devices derived from the **Iris Number System** and the **Master Field Equation** ($\mathcal{D}\mathcal{F}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{J}_{\text{total}}$).

All designs are engineered for rigid mechanical integrity, high-efficiency continuous operation, and fabrication without metal welding, brazing, or specialized industrial machine tools.

## Directory Contents

1. [ADISC-1: Acoustic-Dielectrophoretic Ion Separation & Desalination Cell](adisc_desalination_cell.md)
   - **Mechanism**: Negative dielectrophoretic (nDEP) body force coupled to radial acoustic standing-wave nodes for continuous membrane-free water desalination and particulate separation.
   - **Construction**: Cast reinforced epoxy (with embedded metal rebar / fiber core) or Garolite/Phenolic flanges, clear extruded acrylic tube, flexible interdigitated stator sleeve, piezoelectric Langevin/ring transducer.
   - **Power & Throughput**: $12\text{--}24\text{ V DC}$ solid-state dual-resonance driver ($15\text{ W}$ total), $100\text{--}300\text{ mL/min}$ continuous feed.

2. [MTE-1: Multivector Atmospheric Transpiration Energy Harvester](atmospheric_transpiration_harvester.md)
   - **Mechanism**: Direct thermal-to-electric transduction via asymmetric capillary evaporation and streaming potential double-layer shear.
   - **Construction**: Graded porous terracotta/ceramic matrix, carbonized conductive boundary layers, stainless current collector screens, ambient evaporative cold sink.
   - **Power Output**: Continuous $0.45\text{--}0.7\text{ V DC}$ per unit cell ($5\text{--}12\text{ mW/cm}^2$) powered purely by ambient atmospheric humidity gradients and natural evaporative cooling.

3. [VSH-1: Dual-Resonance Vortex Sonochemical Water Purifier](sonochemical_vortex_purifier.md)

   - **Power & Throughput**: $15\text{ W}$ acoustic power, $1.5\text{--}3.0\text{ L/min}$ continuous gravitational or low-pressure pass-through.

4. [DFE-1: Direct Non-Thermal Field Energy Transducers & Solid-State Power Generators](direct_field_energy_transducers.md)

   - **Construction**: Nanostructured Pd/Ni microstrip lines on AlN/SiC substrates, CVD graphene/hBN asymmetric sawtooth metamaterial arrays, Galfenol/PMN-PT multiferroic resonators, and high-frequency GaN synchronous rectifiers.
   - **Power & Density**: $5\text{--}25\text{ kW/L}$ for the High-Frequency A-Cell; $50\text{--}150\text{ W/m}^2$ for the Asymmetric Metamaterial Harvester; $>88\%\text{--}94\%$ direct electrical efficiency.

5. [MCP-1 & MCP-2: Multi-Stage Capillary-Vapor Pervaporation Purifier & Household Well Decontaminator](multistage_pervaporation_purifier.md)
   - **Mechanism**: Multi-stage micro-gap latent heat recuperation ($N=5\text{--}8$ stages, $d_{\text{gap}} = 0.30\text{ mm}$), localized interfacial photothermal vaporization, counter-current cross-flow self-flushing, and catalytic/redox pre-conditioning for high-efficiency marsh, swamp, and contaminated domestic well water purification.
   - **Construction**: Stacked Aluminum/Copper condenser-evaporator plates with hydrophilic wicks, hydrophobic woven polypropylene micro-mesh gap spacers, food-grade silicone perimeter manifolds, and optional catalytic KDF-55/PTC ceramic core.
   - **Throughput & Efficiency**: $\text{GOR} \approx 3.6\text{--}4.4$; $4.5\text{--}5.8\text{ L}/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{hr})$ under solar insolation (MCP-1) and $3.5\text{--}4.2\text{ L/hr}$ at $0.048\text{ kWh/L}$ for domestic well systems (MCP-2); 100% pathogen, PFAS, nitrate, and heavy metal rejection.
