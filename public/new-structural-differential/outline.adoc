== 1. Physical Representation Components

=== The Plenum (Event Level)
* **Plenum / Sub-microscopic Level**: Configured with magnetic attachments exhibiting South polarity across its surface elements.

=== Objective Levels
* **Fido (Non-human Organism)**: Objective levels represented with non-magnetic pegs or passive attachment interfaces.
* **Smith (Human Organism)**: Objective levels equipped with magnetic attachments exhibiting North polarity, providing selective coupling to the plenum's South poles.

=== Higher Abstractive Levels (Tags / Fabricated Elements)
* **Chained Elements (Replacing Conventional Labels)**: Fabricated elements constructed with a South magnetic pole on one terminal end and a North magnetic pole on the opposing terminal end.
* **Coupling Constraint**: Chaining dynamics enforce that only the distal (far) end of a constructed chain can directly attach to the plenum interface.

== 2. Structural & Architectural Motivation

* **Physical and Objective Capacities**: In analyzing system architectures and information-processing structures (such as automated inference engines and abstract modeling systems), symbolic evaluation alone lacks non-verbal grounding.
* **Grounding on the Objective Level**: Effective operational systems require non-verbal, objective-level structures to precede and anchor symbolic chaining.
* **Primary Capacity Allocation**: The relative scale, connectivity, and structural complexity of the objective levels depicted for Smith in the structural differential underscore that the objective capacities must serve as the primary foundational foundation of the system rather than superficial label manipulation.
