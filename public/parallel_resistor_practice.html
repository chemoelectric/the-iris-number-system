Parallel Resistance Drill: The Conductance Method

Linear Conductance Addition: G_total = G_1 + G_2 + ... + G_n  |  R_total = 1 / G_total  |  Units in Ω and Ω-1
The Two-Step Routine (No Inverted Fractions):
1. Invert each branch to get conductance: G = 1 / R (in Ω-1 or mΩ-1)
2. Sum the conductances: G_total = G_1 + G_2 + ...  →  Invert total: R_total = 1 / G_total
