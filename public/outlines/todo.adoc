* Invariant Foundations of Probability and Gravitational Slowdown of Motion.
  - Bayes' rule as an invariant balance of stuff (rectangles):
    * Asymmetric textbook formulations ("prior", "posterior", "likelihood") distort understanding by making an inherently symmetric identity look like a one-way procedural conveyor belt.
    * Focus on the invariant in the middle:
+
\[ P ( A \mid B ) P ( B ) = P ( A B ) = P ( B \mid A ) P ( A ) \]
+
    * The joint occurrence \( P ( A B ) \) is the invariant, conserved quantity of "stuff" (area). The equation always balances on a scale because both pans weigh the exact same amount of stuff.
    * Investigating an analytic method: treating conditional probability statements as area-preserving projections or transformations from the invariant joint core \( P ( A B ) \) (like squashing and stretching a fixed patch of dough across orthogonal measuring axes).
    * Contrast with John S. Bell: mathematics requires carefully designed definitions. Bell imported an ungrounded colloquial assumption that joint distributions across correlated wave emissions must factorize, breaking the invariant and inventing pseudo-paradoxes out of an algebraic blunder.
  - Gravitation as the actual physical slowdown of moving parts (not kinematic "time dilation"):
    * Special Relativity's claim of symmetric kinematic "velocity time dilation" in empty space is rejected as an ungrounded coordinate artifact.
    * Gravitational time dilation is an _actual physical slowing down of motion_.
    * A clock is made entirely of moving parts: gears, pendulums, quartz crystals, or oscillating electron wave packets and atomic hyperfine transitions in a cesium beam.
    * Near a mass concentration, the local elastic stiffness and wave speed of the plenum \( c ( r ) \) are reduced:
+
\[ c ( r ) = c _ 0 \left( 1 - \frac{G M}{c _ 0 ^ 2 r} \right) \]
+
    * The moving parts of the clock are physically slowed down by the altered medium, completing fewer cycles per unit of duration.
    * An atomic clock in circular orbit measures the local gravitational potential directly in its cabin; its exact physical position across the cabin matters because of the radial wave speed gradient \( \nabla c ( r ) \).
    * Aligns with the experimental realizations of gravitation researchers like C. S. Unnikrishnan: clocks slow down not from abstract relative speed, but from actual physical gravitational potentials and contact stresses in the plenum.


* Definition of Pace \( t _ p \) and the Elimination of Objectified “Time Dilation”.
  - The coordinate transformation from clock reading \( t \) to pace \( t _ p \):
    * Define the invariant progression coordinate \( t _ p \) (“pace”):

+
\[ d t _ p = \frac{c ( r )}{c _ 0} \, d t \iff d t = n ( r ) \, d t _ p \quad \text{where } n ( r ) = \frac{c _ 0}{c ( r )} \approx 1 + \frac{G M}{c _ 0 ^ 2 r} \]
+
    * Under this coordinate transformation, the pace \( t _ p \) does not change under the influence of gravitation. It progresses uniformly throughout the universe, completely immune to the presence of mass concentrations.
    * The physical slowing down of clocks in a gravitational field is then recognized for what it actually is: gravitation does not alter the progression of coordinate pace \( t _ p \); it alters the physical wave speed \( c ( r ) \) and mechanical oscillation rates of physical clocks relative to that invariant pace.
