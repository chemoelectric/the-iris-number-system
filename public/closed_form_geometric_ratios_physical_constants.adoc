=== Kinetic Pressure as Discrete Momentum Flux Conservation

Consider a planar boundary of area \( A \) normal to the \( x \)-axis. Over a finite observation duration \( \tau \), particles striking the boundary undergo elastic reflection via direct contact action, transferring momentum \( \Delta p_x = 2 ( \Delta m ) v_x \) per collision.

The number of collisions within duration \( \tau \) for particles with velocity component \( v_x > 0 \) is:
\[ \Delta N_c = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{N}{V} \right) A v_x \tau \]
The total momentum transferred to the boundary is:
\[ \Delta P_\text{total} = \sum \Delta p_x = \left( \frac{N}{V} \right) A \tau ( \Delta m ) \langle v_x ^ 2 \rangle \]
The mechanical pressure \( P \) is defined extensionally as the time-averaged normal force per unit area, \( P = \frac{\Delta P_\text{total}}{A \tau} \):
\[ P = \left( \frac{N}{V} \right) ( \Delta m ) \langle v_x ^ 2 \rangle = \rho \langle v_x ^ 2 \rangle \]
By spatial isotropy on the discrete lattice grid \( \mathcal{G}_N \), the mean-square velocity components satisfy \( \langle v_x ^ 2 \rangle = \langle v_y ^ 2 \rangle = \langle v_z ^ 2 \rangle = \frac{1}{3} \langle v ^ 2 \rangle \). Therefore:
\[ P = \frac{1}{3} \rho \langle v ^ 2 \rangle = \frac{2}{3} \frac{N}{V} \left( \frac{1}{2} ( \Delta m ) \langle v ^ 2 \rangle \right) \]
Multiplying by volume \( V \) yields the exact kinetic state identity:
\[ P V = \frac{2}{3} N \langle E_k \rangle \]
where \( \langle E_k \rangle = \frac{1}{2} ( \Delta m ) \langle v ^ 2 \rangle \) is the base-scale mean kinetic energy per particle.
